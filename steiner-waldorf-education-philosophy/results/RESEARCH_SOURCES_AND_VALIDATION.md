## Key Research Findings

### Main Lesson Books
- **Origin:** 1919, Stuttgart, Germany (first Waldorf School)
- **German term:** Epochenhefte
- **Core principle:** Students create illustrated notebooks replacing textbooks
- **Pedagogical mechanism:** Multi-modal learning (visual, kinesthetic, semantic) through artistic expression
- **Assessment:** Ipsative (self-referenced), narrative, developmental rather than comparative
- **Modern adoption:** Expanding in non-Waldorf schools; increasing recognition in educational research
- **Vietnamese alignment:** Supports Luật Giáo dục 2019 emphasis on creativity, student-centered learning, and comprehensive development

### Temperaments in Education
- **Origin:** 1909-1910 lecture cycles by Rudolf Steiner
- **Four types:** Choleric (ego-dominant), Sanguine (astral-dominant), Melancholic (physical body-dominant), Phlegmatic (etheric-dominant)
- **Anthroposophical basis:** Each temperament arises from the balance of fourfold human being (physical body, etheric body, astral body, ego)
- **Application:** Seating arrangements, lesson adaptation, story selection, subject-matter differentiation
- **Teacher role:** Conscious observation and balancing of classroom temperaments without rigid labeling
- **Vietnamese alignment:** Honors individual difference while maintaining community harmony; supports 21st-century skill development
