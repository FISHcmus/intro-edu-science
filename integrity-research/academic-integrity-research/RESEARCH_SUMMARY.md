### AI Integrity Relevance

Both researchers' work is foundational for understanding AI-era academic integrity:

**Lancaster's Contribution:** Established that contract cheating requires systemic institutional and pedagogical responses, not detection-based solutions. This framework directly applies to generative AI challenges.

**Bretag's Contribution:** The Comprehensive Academic Integrity Model (five core elements: Access, Approach, Responsibility, Detail, Support) provides an operational framework that institutions can apply to AI integrity governance.
